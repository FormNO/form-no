How this works

You sign in with the X account you already have — no new account, no wallet, no password here. Your handle is what appears on the wall, so nobody can file under your name.

Writing to a blockchain costs a fee. You do not pay it; the project signs each filing and covers the fee on Base.

Filings go to Base in the order they were made. While the chain is still opening, yours is queued and keeps its number; once it is written, it is a permanent public record that nobody can edit or delete, including us.

One filing per account per day. Signing and objecting are not limited.

We keep your handle, your X account number and the link to your avatar. Nothing else, and we never post for you.
